[size=6][b]Extended Pickup Icons Range[/b][/size]
See samples, supplies, mission items and vehicles from farther away, marked with the game's own icons.

[size=5][b]Back after the October 20 update[/b][/size]
The October 20, 2026 game update broke the old version of this mod, and the loader it relied on no longer works. With the new [b]Boot Mod Loader[/b] it can run again, so the mod has been rebuilt from scratch. It now draws its own markers on screen using the game's icons, and it covers much more than before: mission items, drop-off points, valves and vehicles.

[b]If you used the old version, remove it before installing this one.[/b] Bingus Shared Loader and Mod Options Menu are no longer needed for this mod.

[size=5][b]What gets a marker[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Samples[/b] (common, rare, super), sample containers, Terminid eggs: sample icons, 40 m
[*][b]Ammo boxes, grenade boxes, stims, resupply pod crates[/b]: supply icons, 25 m
[*][b]SSSDs, data drives, black boxes, larva containers[/b]: package / larva icons, 40 m
[*][b]Launch codes, keycards, video cameras and other mission items[/b]: diamond, 40 m
[*][b]Artillery shells, warheads and batteries you carry[/b]: diamond, 25 m
[*][b]Drop-off points and cargo containers[/b]: diamond, 40 m
[*][b]Valves and cranks you turn in missions[/b]: diamond, 40 m
[*][b]Exosuits, FRVs and tanks[/b]: vehicle icons, 100 m
[/list]

[list]
[*]Markers get a little smaller the farther away they are.
[*]Items someone is carrying get no marker.
[*]Items close enough for the game to show its own icon get no marker: samples within 9 m of you (Terminid eggs and Automaton super samples 20 m, sample containers 15 m), supplies within 4 m.
[*]The vehicle you are in, or one a teammate is driving, gets no marker. A parked vehicle shows again after a few seconds.
[*]Destroyed exosuits and FRVs lose their marker.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]
Install [b]HD2 Mod Setting GUI[/b] (Nexus mod 17756) to turn each kind on or off and change its distance while you play. Changes apply within a second. Without it, the mod uses the default distances above.

You can also change how markers look. By default they look as described above.
[list]
[*][b]Hide markers where the game shows its own icon[/b]: turn off to keep markers up close too (useful with first-person camera mods).
[*][b]Marker size at the edge of range[/b]: how small markers get far away.
[*][b]Marker opacity[/b] and [b]Fade markers with distance[/b].
[*][b]Move sample and supply markers[/b] left/right and up/down, to line them up with the game's icons.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Boot Mod Loader[/b] (Nexus mod 17754)
[*]Optional: [b]HD2 Mod Setting GUI[/b] (Nexus mod 17756) to change settings
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game completely. Remove or disable any older version of this mod.
[*]Import this ZIP into your mod manager (Arsenal etc.) together with Boot Mod Loader.
[*][b]Load order: Boot Mod Loader must be below this mod.[/b] In Arsenal, mods listed at the bottom have higher priority.
[*]Deploy, then start the game.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Drop-off points and valves stay marked after you use them. The mod cannot tell whether one is finished.
[*]Destroyed [b]tanks[/b] and [b]supply FRVs[/b] keep their marker until the wreck disappears from the map. Exosuits and other FRVs are recognised as destroyed.
[*]Terminals are not marked; they are easy to spot already.
[/list]

2026년 10월 20일 게임 업데이트로 예전 버전이 작동하지 않게 되었고, 의존하던 로더도 더 이상 쓸 수 없게 되었습니다. 새로운 [b]Boot Mod Loader[/b]로 다시 작동할 수 있게 되어 모드를 처음부터 새로 만들었습니다. 이제 게임 아이콘으로 화면에 직접 마커를 그리며, 임무 아이템, 반납 지점, 밸브, 차량까지 예전보다 훨씬 많은 것을 표시합니다.

[b]예전 버전을 쓰셨다면 먼저 지우고 설치하세요.[/b] 이 모드는 더 이상 Bingus Shared Loader와 Mod Options Menu가 필요하지 않습니다.

[size=5][b]설정[/b][/size]

마커 모양도 바꿀 수 있습니다. 기본값은 위 설명 그대로입니다.
[list]
[*][b]게임 아이콘이 뜨는 곳에서 마커 숨기기[/b]: 끄면 가까이에서도 마커가 남습니다(1인칭 카메라 모드와 함께 쓸 때 유용).
[*][b]범위 끝에서의 마커 크기[/b]: 멀리 있을 때 마커가 얼마나 작아질지.
[*][b]마커 투명도[/b]와 [b]거리에 따라 흐려지기[/b].
[*][b]샘플·보급품 마커 위치 이동[/b](좌우·상하): 게임 아이콘과 위치를 맞출 때.
[/list]
