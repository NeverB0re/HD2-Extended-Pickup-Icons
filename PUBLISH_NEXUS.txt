[size=6][b]Extended Pickup Icons Range[/b][/size]
See samples, supplies, mission items and vehicles from farther away, marked with the game's own icons.

[size=5][b]Back after the October 20 update[/b][/size]
The October 20, 2026 game update broke the old version of this mod, and the loader it relied on no longer works. With the new [b]Chef Shared Loader[/b] it can run again, so the mod has been rebuilt from scratch. It now draws its own markers on screen using the game's icons, and it covers much more than before: mission items, drop-off points, valves and vehicles.

[b]If you used the old version, remove it before installing this one.[/b] Bingus Shared Loader and Mod Options Menu are no longer needed for this mod.

[size=5][b]What gets a marker[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Samples[/b], 50 m: common, rare and super samples, sample containers, Terminid eggs (sample icons)
[*][b]Supplies[/b], 25 m: ammo boxes, grenade boxes, stims, resupply pod crates (supply icons)
[*][b]Mission items[/b], 50 m: SSSDs, data drives, black boxes and larva containers (sky blue package and larva icons); launch codes, keycards, video cameras, artillery shells, warheads and other things you carry (diamonds)
[*][b]Mission objectives[/b], 50 m: drop-off points, cargo containers, valves and cranks (diamonds)
[*][b]Hellbombs[/b], 100 m: the hellbombs lying about the map that blow up when shot (red hellbomb icon)
[*][b]Vehicles[/b], 100 m: exosuits, FRVs and tanks (sky blue vehicle icons)
[/list]

[list]
[*]Markers get smaller the farther away they are, and supply markers fainter. Vehicle markers also show how far away they are.
[*]Ammo, grenade, stim, package and egg icons are drawn from the game's own textures.
[*]Markers sit where the game shows its own icon, also on samples that grow on stalks and ones shot loose.
[*]Vehicles out of view show at the edge of the screen, with an arrow pointing to them.
[*]Picked-up launch codes and keycards, hellbombs that blew up and destroyed vehicles lose their marker.
[*]Markers follow the game's camera, also when you zoom in with a scope.
[*]On your ship, mission markers are off.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]
Press [b]F9[/b] in the game to open Chef Shared Loader's options menu, then pick [b]Extended Pickup Icons Range[/b]. Each of the six kinds above can be turned on or off and given its own distance while you play. Also there:
[list]
[*][b]Fade supplies far away[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Show distance above vehicles[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Hide markers where the game shows its own icon[/b]: turn off to keep markers up close too (useful with first-person camera mods).
[*][b]Marker size far away[/b] and [b]Marker opacity[/b].
[/list]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Chef Shared Loader[/b] (Nexus mod 17760), 1.2.0 or newer
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game completely. Remove or disable any older version of this mod.
[*]Import this ZIP into your mod manager (Arsenal etc.) together with Chef Shared Loader.
[*][b]Load order: Chef Shared Loader must have the highest priority.[/b] In Arsenal with "Last mod highest priority" (the default), put it at the bottom of the list.
[*]Purge and Deploy, then start the game.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Limits[/b][/size]
Since the October 20 update, the game runs mods in a locked-down Lua with anti-cheat: a mod can no longer read the game's memory. Chef Shared Loader gives mods where things are and lets them draw on screen, but not what state they are in: whether an item was picked up, a valve is finished, a vehicle is destroyed or who is in it. So the mod works these out from where things are and which of their parts show, and can now and then be wrong:
[list]
[*][b]Carried items[/b]: an item right on a helldiver is taken for carried and gets no marker.
[*][b]Items put into a drop-off point, shells loaded into artillery[/b]: the item stays there once put in, so one right at a drop-off point or ready rack is taken for delivered and gets no marker. The drop-off point keeps its own, as some missions have you take things out of one again.
[*][b]Up close[/b]: the mod cannot hide the game's own icons, so it hides its marker where the game shows one: samples within 9 m of you (Terminid eggs and Automaton super samples 20 m, sample containers 15 m), ammo and grenade boxes within 4 m. Stims have no icon of the game's and keep theirs.
[*][b]Vehicles in use[/b]: the mod cannot tell who is in a vehicle. One a helldiver is in that is moving gets no marker; one that has stood still for a few seconds is taken for parked and shows again.
[*][b]Destroyed vehicles[/b]: the mod cannot read a vehicle's health. A vehicle whose gun is gone or has come off is taken for destroyed, once it stands still.
[*][b]Valves and cranks[/b] stay marked after you turn them: whether one is finished can't be told.
[/list]

[*][b]먼 곳의 마커 크기[/b], [b]마커 불투명도[/b].
[/list]

[size=5][b]한계점[/b][/size]
10월 20일 업데이트 이후 게임은 안티치트와 함께 제한된 Lua 환경에서 모드를 실행하고, 모드는 더 이상 게임 메모리를 읽을 수 없습니다. Chef Shared Loader는 물체가 어디에 있는지와 화면에 그리는 기능은 주지만, 물체의 상태(아이템을 주웠는지, 밸브를 다 돌렸는지, 차량이 파괴됐는지, 누가 타고 있는지)는 알려 주지 않습니다. 그래서 모드는 이런 것들을 위치와 모델의 어느 부분이 보이는지로 추정하며, 가끔 틀릴 수 있습니다.
[list]
[*][b]들고 있는 아이템[/b]: 헬다이버 몸에 붙어 있는 아이템은 누군가 들고 있는 것으로 보고 마커를 띄우지 않습니다.
[*][b]반납 지점에 넣은 아이템, 야포에 장전한 포탄[/b]: 넣은 뒤에도 물체가 그 자리에 남기 때문에, 반납 지점이나 포탄 거치대 바로 옆에 있으면 넣은 것으로 보고 마커를 띄우지 않습니다. 반납 지점에서 다시 꺼내 옮기는 임무가 있어서 반납 지점 자체의 마커는 남깁니다.
[*][b]사용 중인 차량[/b]: 누가 타고 있는지 알 수 없어서, 헬다이버가 탄 채 움직이는 차량에는 마커를 띄우지 않습니다. 몇 초 동안 멈춰 있으면 주차한 것으로 보고 다시 표시합니다.
[*][b]파괴된 차량[/b]: 차량 체력을 읽을 수 없어서, 달린 총이 사라지거나 떨어져 나간 차량은 멈춰 선 뒤 파괴된 것으로 봅니다.
[*][b]밸브와 크랭크[/b]: 완료 여부를 알 수 없어서 돌린 뒤에도 표시됩니다.
[/list]
